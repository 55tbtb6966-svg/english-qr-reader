English QR Reader v4.5.37-data-prep

Purpose:
- Prepare data before implementing the QR sentence-building tab.
- Keep existing A/B/C/N/P/S behavior unchanged.

Changes:
1) W is reserved for sentence parts.
   - Initial master: W_sentence_parts_master.csv
   - 166 unique visible forms, W000001-W000166.
   - Duplicate visible forms (e.g. you, it, her, when, so) are one W ID with multiple grammatical roles in Explanation.

2) RelatedForms support added as OPTIONAL 17th CSV column.
   - Legacy first 16 columns remain in the same order.
   - Existing CSV without RelatedForms imports normally; RelatedForms becomes blank.
   - Export writes RelatedForms as the last column.
   - Data editor can edit RelatedForms.
   - Search also includes RelatedForms.

Recommended verb format:
3sg=has|past=had|pastParticiple=had|ing=having

3) A master actual data is NOT bundled in the app ZIP.
   - A_master_relatedforms_template.csv shows the new schema and an example.
   - To migrate the real A master safely, import the existing A CSV into this build and export it; the original 16 columns are preserved and RelatedForms is appended.
   - Verb forms can then be filled into RelatedForms progressively.

4) QR sentence-building UI is intentionally NOT included yet.
